Helpful Before You Enquire

A Few Things You May Want to Know

Every ThreadMark piece is personal. Here are a few helpful answers before you share your idea with us.

What details should I include when requesting a quote?

Share the product you have in mind, the embroidery details, estimated quantity, deadline, and any preferred colors or placement. A reference image, sketch, logo, or photo is always helpful.

Can I order a single personalized piece?

Yes. ThreadMark welcomes one-off personalized orders for gifts, home pieces, apparel, and meaningful keepsakes, subject to the product and design requirements.

Can you embroider my logo or artwork?

Yes. Upload your logo, artwork, or reference file with your enquiry. We will review it and help confirm suitable sizing, placement, thread colors, and embroidery options.

How long will my custom order take?

Timelines depend on design complexity, quantity, product availability, and approval timing. Once we understand your request, we will share an estimated completion date before work begins.

Do you take bulk, corporate, and event orders?

Yes. We can help with branded apparel, teamwear, uniforms, event gifting, wedding sets, and coordinated embroidered pieces. Include your quantity and required date in the quote form.

Can you help me choose the right product and thread colors?

Yes. If you are unsure about the product, embroidery placement, lettering, or thread colors, share your occasion or idea with us and we will suggest a suitable direction.
