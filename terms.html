Custom & Personalized Orders

Personalized products are created according to the information, artwork, names, initials, colors, placement instructions, quantities, and other details provided or approved by the customer.

Customers are responsible for reviewing the information they provide before production begins. This includes spelling, names, initials, dates, artwork, logo details, quantities, and other personalization instructions.

Personalization requires accuracy.

Please carefully review all custom information before approving an order. Once personalization or production has started, some changes may no longer be possible.

04

Artwork & Design Submissions

When submitting logos, artwork, graphics, names, monograms, or other design materials, you should ensure that you have the appropriate permission or authority to use the submitted material for the requested purpose.

Submitted artwork may need to be adjusted, digitized, resized, simplified, or otherwise prepared so that it can be reproduced through embroidery.

Provide the highest-quality artwork available whenever possible.

Clearly specify preferred colors, dimensions, placement, and personalization details.

Review any design proof or embroidery preview provided before giving approval.

Prices for custom embroidery may depend on the selected product, quantity, design complexity, embroidery size, stitch requirements, personalization, materials, and other order details.

A quotation is based on the information available when it is prepared. If the specifications, quantities, artwork, materials, or requested services change, the quotation may also need to be updated.

Custom Pricing Every project is quoted according to its requirements.

Bulk and corporate orders may have different pricing structures based on quantity, customization, and production requirements.

Design Approval

Where a design proof, sample, digital preview, or other approval step is provided, customers should carefully review the design before approving production.

Approval indicates that the customer has reviewed the relevant details shown in the proof or agreed specifications, including spelling, layout, placement, sizing, and other visible customization details.

Delivery arrangements may vary depending on the order and destination.

Need to make a change?
