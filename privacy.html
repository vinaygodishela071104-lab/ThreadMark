01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ThreadMark collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, upload a custom design, or communicate with us about our embroidery and monogramming services.

By using our website or providing information through our forms, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with ThreadMark and the services you request.

Contact information: your name, email address, phone number, and other details you provide when contacting us.

Order and enquiry information: product preferences, quantities, personalization details, delivery requirements, and quotation information.

Business information: company or organization details provided for corporate and bulk embroidery enquiries.

Website information: basic technical information that may be collected when you access and interact with our website.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to ThreadMark may be used to respond to your requests and provide our personalization services.

Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.

Understand your embroidery, monogramming, or personalization requirements.

Communicate about custom designs, bulk orders, corporate requirements, and related services.

Improve our website, customer experience, and services.

Maintain appropriate business and communication records.

04

Custom Designs & Uploads

When you provide artwork, logos, names, initials, monograms, reference images, or other design information, we use those materials to understand and prepare your requested personalization.

Your designs deserve care.

Custom artwork and personalization details are intended to be used in connection with your enquiry or requested ThreadMark service.

05

Information Sharing

ThreadMark does not treat personal information as a product for sale. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate our business, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect legitimate rights and interests.

Where third-party service providers assist with functions such as communication, website operations, delivery, or other business services, they may receive information necessary to perform those functions.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no website, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Cookies & Website Preferences

Our website may use browser storage or similar technologies where necessary to remember website preferences and support functionality.

For example, ThreadMark currently uses browser storage to remember interface preferences such as dark mode and text direction so that your selected experience can remain consistent between pages.

08

Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may be able to contact us to ask about personal information you previously provided, request that inaccurate information be corrected, or raise a privacy-related concern.

We may need reasonable information to understand and verify a request before responding.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, business practices, or applicable requirements change. When we make updates, the revised version may be published on this page with an updated revision date.
